Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding credential verification, downloads, and institutional acceptance

Will my college or university accept this internship certificate?
Yes. Elevatifier certificates are ISO 9001:2015 accredited and endorsed by institutional recognitions including DPIIT and Startup India. They include an authentic scannable QR code and unique serial ID recognized for college academic credit requirements.
How does the QR Code and verification link work?
Every certificate is issued a unique serial ID (e.g. ELV-2026-XXXXXX) and an active dynamic QR code. Scanning the QR code with any smartphone camera instantly opens the official registry at https://certify.elevatifier.com/verify/ID, verifying authenticity and candidate details.
Can I change my name or domain after making the payment?
No. To prevent tampering and fraud, certificates are cryptographically locked and permanently sealed into the registry upon payment. Please ensure your name and details are completely accurate before proceeding to payment.
How will the certificate get saved in my mobile gallery?
Upon payment completion, click the "Download Certificate (Image)" button. The high-resolution 300 DPI PNG file will download immediately into your device's photo gallery or downloads folder.
Is the ₹399 payment a one-time fee? Any hidden costs?
Yes, ₹399 is a one-time all-inclusive fee. It covers the high-res 300 DPI image generation, scannable dynamic QR code, permanent registry hosting on MongoDB Atlas, and lifetime public verification with zero extra charges.
How long is the certificate valid for college credit evaluation?
Elevatifier credentials have lifetime validity. Your unique certificate record is permanently locked in our cloud database, ensuring any university faculty or background verification team can verify it anytime 24/7.
